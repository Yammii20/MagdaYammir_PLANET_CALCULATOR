Cosmic Calculation Logic

A "year" is simply the time it takes a planetary body to complete one full orbit around its star. Earth takes approximately 365.25 solar days. Faster inner worlds like Mercury complete their orbits in just 88 days, resulting in higher numerical ages. Outer gas giants like Neptune take over 160 Earth years to orbit once!

* Pluto is proudly retained in our nine-world solar system exploration, representing its full 248 Earth-year orbital revolution.
